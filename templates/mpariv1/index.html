About mParivahan

mParivahan is an initiative by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India, developed by the National Informatics Centre (NIC).

Key Features

  • Digital RC: Access and show your RC digitally.
  • Digital DL: Store and display your driving license.
  • Challan Management: View, pay, and dispute traffic challans.
  • RTO Services: Apply for learner's license, renew license.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mParivahan app free?
Yes, the mParivahan app is completely free.
Are digital documents valid?
Yes, as per the IT Act, 2000.
